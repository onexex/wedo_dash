Step 1 · The applicant

The Apply page walks them through six screens

1  Position

Picks one of the open positions HR has posted.

2  About you

Name, birth date, civil status, plus an optional live camera photo.

3  Contact

Full address, Philippine mobile number and email.

4  Education

Schools, government IDs (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, TIN…) and last job.

5  Family

Family members and who to call in an emergency.

6  Resume

Uploads a PDF or Word file, checks the summary and certifies it is true.

When they submit, the applicant gets an automatic “Application received” email.

Step 1 · Keeping the public form safe

Real applicants get through, spam does not

3

applications per hour from one internet address (60 per hour overall)

20s

minimum time to fill in the form; an open form expires after 2 hours

30

days before the same mobile number or resume file can apply again

5 MB

resume limit, PDF or Word only, checked by its real content

Names are letters only, links are refused, and so are throwaway email addresses.

Resumes and photos are stored privately; only HR can open them, and every view is logged.

Step 3 · HR

What HR sees in the Applicant Pool

Full profile

Everything from the six screens, plus the resume and photo.

Rehire check

Matches the name against former employees and flags do not rehire or review.

Stage filters

Counts per stage, filter by position, search by name or email.

Notes & history

Internal notes on each move. Notes are never emailed.

Openings

HR posts, opens and closes the positions people can apply for.

Emails

Optional job offer or not selected email, with a message from HR.

The Applicant Pool also works in the WeDo phone app, and resumes open on the phone.

Step 4 · One-click hire

HR fills in the job; WeDo does the rest

HR enters

CompanyDepartment PositionImmediate superior AgencyHMO Classification: TraineeHire date Monthly basicAllowance Hourly rateID number

Available only at the Job offer stage.

WeDo does, all at once

  • Creates the employee no.: company code + next number, e.g. ABC-0153
  • Makes a username from the name and a one-time password, shown to HR once
  • Copies the 201: profile, IDs, education, family, emergency contacts, pay
  • Makes the camera photo the profile picture
  • Marks the applicant Hired and records it in the employee’s history
  • Sends a welcome email with employee no. and username (never the password)

The password is never emailed, so only HR and the new hire ever see it.
